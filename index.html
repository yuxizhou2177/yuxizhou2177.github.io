01About me

I work at the intersection of policy, implementation and communication.

I am Yuxi. I work at UNESCO in Paris on the International Convention against Doping in Sport (2005). Much of my work starts with a national report — understanding what a country has put in place, where the implementation gap is, and what kind of support could realistically help move things forward.

Together with my colleague Rinrada, we monitor how States Parties implement the Convention and help turn those findings into practical follow-up — from technical guidance and capacity-building to policy dialogue, knowledge products and communication tools.

One thing I have learned is that an implementation gap does not necessarily mean a lack of commitment. In my work with SIDS and LDCs, constraints are often practical: limited staff, fragmented responsibilities, competing priorities and limited implementation capacity. This has made me curious about what actually helps turn commitments into something that works in practice.

I also translate complex policy, technical and data-driven information into dashboards, briefs, infographics, web content and videos — because information is most useful when it is clear, accessible and actionable.

I love the moment when numbers start to tell a story, and when a good visual says what a long paragraph cannot.

02Project

SIDSight

Data
Agrifood policy explorer for 8 SIDS · August 2026

An independent mini-project I built around a practical question: if I had a bilateral meeting with a SIDS Permanent Mission tomorrow, what would I want to understand tonight?

SIDSight brings together 8 SIDS across the Pacific, Caribbean and AIS regions, combining indicators on food import exposure, agriculture and fisheries, land availability, climate vulnerability and digital connectivity into a simple tool for country comparison and meeting preparation.

A little more

Across three pages, it moves from a cross-country overview, to a country brief, and finally to relevant FAO programmes — from “what is the situation?” to “where could the conversation go?”

The Journey of the Anti-Doping Convention

Live
Interactive board · July – October 2025

An interactive board developed for COP10 to tell twenty years of the Convention through key milestones, key projects, impactful data and impact.

I served as the UNESCO focal point, coordinating the project from concept to launch with India’s Ministry of Youth Affairs and Sport.

The challenge was turning two decades of institutional history into an interactive story, making the Convention’s evolution accessible and engaging for an international audience.

Short video for COP10 · 2025

A short video marking twenty years of the Convention, tracing key milestones in chronological order. I developed the concept, script and full edit, bringing together major moments from two decades of the Convention in a concise and accessible format.
