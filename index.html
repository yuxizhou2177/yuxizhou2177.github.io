01About me

I turn substance into communication.

At UNESCO in Paris, my work spans Member State monitoring under the International Convention against Doping in Sport, alongside coordination of a global initiative on Traditional Pharmacopoeia. Communication runs across both: I develop briefing notes, articles, web content, visuals and digital products for senior management, Member States and international audiences.

I enjoy turning complex information into clear, useful communication — finding the right angle, shaping the message and adapting it to the audience.
